01 — Overview

What is Sovereign Hub?

Sovereign Hub is a private capital syndicate platform that connects early-stage and growing businesses with verified investors. It's built around one belief: a business shouldn't die just because the founder didn't know the right people.

Unlike traditional funding routes — where access depends on connections, geography, or luck — Sovereign Hub is structured, transparent, and accessible to anyone with a real business and a compelling pitch. Think of it as Shark Tank — but open, fair, and built for real businesses.

Core Philosophy
The platform handles proposals, offers, negotiations, and deal records — all in one auditable place. No middlemen. No backroom deals. Full transparency for both sides of the table.
🔍
Full Transparency
Every proposal, offer, and deal is logged and auditable. No hidden terms, no surprises.
⚖️
Structured Negotiation
Offers include equity percentage, valuation, and investment amount — clearly defined every time.
🛡️
Role-Based Access
Founders and Investors see only what's relevant to their role. Secure by design.
💬
Direct Messaging
Once a deal is in play, both sides can communicate directly — right inside the platform.
02 — Getting Started

Creating Your Account

Signing up takes under 2 minutes. You'll choose your role at the start — this determines what you can do on the platform.

1
Visit the Auth Page
Click "Join Now" from the homepage. You'll be taken to the authentication terminal.
2
Choose Your Role
Select Founder if you have a business, or Investor (Shark) if you have capital. This cannot be changed later.
3
Register with Email
Enter your full name, email, and a secure password. Verify your email to activate your account.
4
Complete Your Profile
Fill in your bio, location, and role-specific details. A complete profile builds trust with the other side.
5
Access Your Dashboard
You'll be redirected to your personalised dashboard — your command centre for everything on the platform.

🚀 For Founders 04 — Proposals

Submitting Your Proposal

Your proposal is your pitch to every investor on the platform. Navigate to Submit Pitch in the nav after logging in.

1
Business Title & Tagline
A clear name and one-line tagline that instantly communicates your value proposition.
2
Category & Location
Select your industry (Tech, Food, Health, etc.) and your operating location.
3
Funding Target & Equity
State how much capital you need (₹) and what equity percentage you're offering in exchange.
4
Business Description
What problem you solve, your traction, team, and why you need the funding now.
5
Submit for Review
Your proposal enters pending state and appears in the investor marketplace immediately.
💡 Tips for a Strong Proposal
Be specific about traction — even early numbers matter. Explain why you chose the equity percentage you did. A clear, honest proposal scores higher and attracts better investors.

🦈 For Investors 07 — Making Offers

Structuring Your Offer

Visit any proposal in the Marketplace and click "Make an Offer." You fill in three fields:

₹
Investment Amount
How much capital you're offering. Can be less than the total target — multiple investors can co-fund a proposal.
%
Equity Percentage
The stake you want in exchange. The platform shows the founder's asked equity so you can align your offer.
💬
Message to Founder
Optional but recommended. Explain your interest and any value-add beyond capital that you bring.
⚡ Offer Status Flow
Your offer shows as Pending until the founder responds. If accepted, a deal is created. If declined, you're free to make offers on other proposals.

🤝 Both Roles 10 — Messaging

The Chat System

Sovereign Hub has a built-in messaging system so founders and investors can communicate directly — no emails, no external tools needed.

Access it from the Messages link in the navigation bar after logging in.

Who can I message?
Founders can message investors who have sent them offers. Investors can message founders whose proposals they've made offers on. Once a deal is closed, both parties have full access to the chat.

12 — FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I change my role after signing up? +
No — your role (Founder or Investor) is set at registration and cannot be changed. If you need a different role, create a new account with a different email.
Can multiple investors fund the same proposal? +
Yes. A proposal can receive multiple offers and multiple deals can be created if a founder accepts more than one — for example, two investors splitting the funding target.
What happens after a deal is closed? +
A deal record is created on the platform with both parties' details. The funding is logged. The real-world transaction happens off-platform — both sides then use the chat to coordinate.
Is my information safe? +
Sovereign Hub uses Supabase with Row-Level Security (RLS) policies. This means you can only access your own data — no other user can see your private information unless they're part of a shared deal.
Can I edit my proposal after submitting? +
Yes — navigate to your proposal from the Dashboard and click the edit icon. You can update the description, equity, and funding target. Proposals with active offers cannot be deleted.
What is the Success Score? +
The Success Score (0–100) is a platform rating assigned to proposals based on completeness and detail quality. A higher score makes your proposal more prominent in the marketplace.
Does Sovereign Hub take a commission? +
The platform is currently in its growth phase. All current deals are commission-free. Fee structures will be announced separately when introduced.
13 — Glossary

Platform Terminology

New to startup investing? Here's a quick glossary of terms used across Sovereign Hub.

Proposal
A business pitch submitted by a Founder. Contains business details, funding target, and equity offered.
Offer
A formal investment bid made by an Investor on a Proposal — includes amount, equity %, and valuation.
Deal
A closed agreement between a Founder and Investor. Created when an Offer is accepted.
Equity
Ownership percentage in a company offered to an investor in exchange for capital.
Valuation
The implied total value of a business, derived from the investment amount and equity percentage.
Syndicate
A group of investors who collectively back ventures. Sovereign Hub operates as a digital syndicate.
Shark
Platform term for an Investor — inspired by the bold, decisive nature of capital deployers.
Success Score
A 0–100 rating given to proposals based on quality and completeness. Higher = more visibility.
Funding Log
A record of all capital deployed on the platform, tied to specific proposals and deals.
RLS
Row-Level Security — the database policy ensuring each user only accesses their own data.
